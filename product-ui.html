A closer look at interfaces I’ve designed and shipped, including workflow branches, role permissions, calculations and asset navigation.

Budibase

Automation branches

A workflow can follow different paths depending on its conditions. Someone building it needs to understand what sends a run down each path and how those paths connect to the rest of the workflow.

I designed branch steps with conditions attached to each path. This kept the logic visible on the canvas, so builders could follow the routes and see where to add another condition.

Budibase

Role permissions

Custom roles let administrators decide what people can access and do within an application. The existing interface presented permissions as a flat list of toggles, which was difficult to scan.

I reorganised the permissions around Read, Write and Execute levels. This gave administrators a clearer structure for reviewing what each role could do.

I also placed role assignment beside the roles in settings.

Budibase

View calculations

Builders sometimes needed calculated values alongside the data already in their views. I brought spreadsheet-style formulas into the view builder, with JavaScript available for more complex calculations. Results updated within the view, so builders could check a calculation against real rows while working on it.

Perforce · P4 DAM

Advanced filtering

When several filters were active, users needed to understand why particular assets appeared in their results. The existing interface kept that information inside a panel, so they had to reopen it to check their selections.

I brought the active filters above the results as removable chips. Users could see the current query and remove a filter without losing sight of the assets.

Perforce · P4 DAM

Dashboard

People returning to P4 DAM often wanted to continue working on an asset or review. The previous home screen made them navigate further into the product to find that work.

I redesigned the dashboard to bring recent files, active reviews and search forward. This gave users more direct starting points for continuing their work or finding another asset.

Perforce · P4 DAM

Breadcrumbs

P4 DAM organises assets within folder hierarchies. As users moved through them, they needed to understand their location and find their way to other folders.

Navigation patterns differed across the product. I standardised the breadcrumbs, including how long paths were shortened and how users moved to parent or sibling folders.
