Selected work

Product UI collection

A selection of interfaces I’ve designed to help people build workflows, manage access, work with data and find their files.

Budibase

Automation branches

Workflows sometimes need to take different routes. I designed a way for people to add conditions to each route and see them beside the path they control. This made it easier to understand what would happen when the workflow ran.

Budibase

Role permissions

Administrators need to control what people can see and do in an app. The old settings were a long list of switches, which made permissions difficult to check.

I grouped them into Read, Write and Execute: viewing information, changing it and running actions. I also brought role assignment into the same settings area.

Budibase

View calculations

I designed a way for people to calculate values within their data, using formulas similar to a spreadsheet. They could see the results alongside their existing rows and check them as they worked. JavaScript was also available for more complex calculations.

Perforce · P4 DAM

Advanced filtering

People had to reopen the filter panel to check which filters were affecting their results.

I moved the selected filters above the results, where they stayed visible. Users could see what was applied and remove individual filters without opening the panel again.

Perforce · P4 DAM

Dashboard

I redesigned the home screen to help people get back to their work. Recent files, ongoing reviews and search were easier to reach, so users could pick up where they left off without digging through the product.

Perforce · P4 DAM

Folder navigation

People needed to know where they were as they moved through folders. I made the navigation trail consistent across the product, showing their current location and giving them a way to move back or switch to a nearby folder.

I also defined how longer folder paths should display without taking up too much space.
